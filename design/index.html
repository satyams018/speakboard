Three directions for SpeakBoard

Each page is clickable: tap pictures, press Speak, and switch to Hindi or Spanish. They use the app's real words and grammar; only the look differs.

A. Laminate

Looks like the laminated picture cards families and therapists already use, stuck on a velcro sentence strip. Thick frames show the word type. Familiar, playful, instantly "AAC".

Open Laminate

B. Voice

The person's sentence is the hero, in huge type, and each word lights up as it is spoken. Clean and modern; strongest in a demo video.

Open Voice

C. Calm

Low stimulation for autistic and sensory-sensitive users: deep indigo, soft colours, a slow glow while speaking. Speak and the sentence sit at the bottom, within thumb reach.

Open Calm
